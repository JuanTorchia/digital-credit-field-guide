### Introduction

Apyx is bringing **Digital Credit** onchain.

Following our launch on Solana, we’re challenging creators to explain why dividend-backed yield powered by publicly traded preferred equity could become one of crypto’s next major asset classes.

Whether you’re passionate about DeFi, RWAs, or onchain finance, we want your help educating the Solana community on Digital Credit and how Apyx makes it accessible.

### Mission

Traditional credit markets represent trillions of dollars in value, but they’ve remained largely inaccessible onchain.

Apyx changes that by giving users access to **Digital Credit**, dividend-backed yield sourced from publicly traded preferred equity like Strategy’s **$STRC** and Strive’s **$SATA**.

Our goal is simple: help more people understand this emerging asset class and why Apyx is building the infrastructure to bring it onchain.
